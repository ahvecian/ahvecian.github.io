About

I am a Professor of Economics at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My research is in microeconomic theory, with a focus on auctions, mechanism design and information economics. My work has appeared in journals including the Journal of Economic Theory, Games and Economic Behavior and the Review of Financial Studies.

I currently direct the Master in Economics of Competition, Regulation and Markets (CReMa) and teach Principles of Economics (undergraduate), Microeconomics III (PhD) alongside massive open online courses on the fundamentals of micro and macroeconomics.

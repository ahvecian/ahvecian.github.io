Research projects

I have led and participated in competitive research projects funded by Spanish national and regional agencies for over two decades. My research focuses on mechanism design, market design, industrial organization, auctions, information economics, and decentralized finance.

As Principal Investigator, I have led projects on mechanism design and benchmark design, digital finance and decentralized oracles, information diffusion, competition in bidding markets, and auction theory. I have also collaborated in large research projects led by scholars including Antonio Cabrales, Fernando Vega-Redondo, Juan José Dolado, Francesco De Sinopoli, and Luis C. Corchón, covering topics such as information, incentives, social networks, industrial organization, and market institutions.

Overall, I have participated in more than fifteen competitively funded research projects supported by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation, the Community of Madrid, the Generalitat Valenciana, and the Instituto Valenciano de Investigaciones Econòmiques.

Conference presentations

I have presented my research at leading international conferences and workshops in economics, including meetings of the Econometric Society, the Society for the Advancement of Economic Theory (SAET), the European Economic Association (EEA), the Society for Economic Design (SED), and the European Association for Research in Industrial Economics (EARIE).

My work has been presented at conferences and invited workshops held in the United States, the United Kingdom, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Portugal, China, Vietnam, Denmark, Belgium, Greece, and Spain. Research topics include auction theory, mechanism design, bargaining, industrial organization, financial markets, benchmark manipulation, and information economics.

Overall, I have delivered more than 25 presentations, including several invited seminars and workshops at leading universities and research institutions.
